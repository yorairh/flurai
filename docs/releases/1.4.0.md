# FluRai 1.4.0

Grammar is now taken one lesson at a time.

**New**
- **Next lesson.** Each level's Grammar screen starts with a "Next lesson" card, so you always know what to learn next. After a lesson, its page offers the next one.
- **I've learned this.** A lesson now counts as learned when you tap this button on its page (or when you answer a question about it). You no longer have to practice it first. "Known" still takes 3 right answers.
- **N5 in teaching order.** N5 points now go from です and the first particles, through verbs, adjectives and wishes, to the て-form and plain forms. N4 to N1 keep their old order for now.

**Changed**
- **Practice only asks about what you learned.** "Practice what you learned" draws questions from the points you have started, and is off until your first lesson. Placement tests and level checkpoints still cover the whole level.

**Good to know**
- Points you already practiced count as learned, so nothing is lost.
- Your progress, decks and settings are untouched, and backup works as before.
