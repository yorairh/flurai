# FluRai 0.15.0

Furigana in Reading.

- Every kanji word in a sentence shows its reading in small kana above it. Kana inside the word (the べ in 食べ物) gets no reading, so the reading lines up with the kanji only.
- A "Show furigana" switch at the top of Reading turns it off; the choice is remembered and backed up with your other settings.
- Tapping an underlined word still opens its meaning and the button to add it to your flashcards.
