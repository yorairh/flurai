# FluRai 0.17.0

Custom decks.

- **My decks:** Learn → Flashcards now has a "My decks" section. Make a deck with New deck, open it to see its words, rename it, delete it, or remove single words.
- **Add words from anywhere:** every word page has an "Add to a deck" button (tick the decks it belongs to, or make a new deck right there). On a title in Explore → Media, "Save them as a deck" puts the title's top words into a new deck.
- **Study one deck:** "Study this deck" runs the normal flashcard session, but only with that deck's words: its due reviews and its new words (still limited by your new words per day). Cards are shared with your other decks, so a word's progress is the same wherever you study it.
- Deleting a deck only deletes the list; the words keep their flashcards and progress.
- Decks are backed up with your account like channels (restored on a fresh install), kept locally for guests, and move over with the rest of your progress when a guest creates an account.
