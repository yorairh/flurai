# FluRai 1.9.10

**Fixed (furigana)**
- A word with several kanji now shows one reading over each kanji (月曜日 shows げつ, よう, び), not one block of kana at the start. This works for all words, on flashcards, the word page and example sentences. Words read as a whole (今日, 大人) keep a single reading.
- A single word is centered over its kanji, and a narrow first reading in a sentence is centered too.
