# FluRai 0.23.0

Release prep: a review of the whole app before sharing it with friends. Mostly fixes you won't see, plus a few you will.

- **Cloud backup fix:** a card reviewed between two backups was sometimes never uploaded (the backup saw its own previous upload and skipped the newer review). The same could happen to grammar progress and study time. Now whichever copy is newer always ends up in the cloud, so a new phone restores your latest progress.
- **Backup timing uses the server's clock:** two phones with slightly different clocks no longer miss each other's changes.
- **Words you add yourself are backed up right away:** a word added to your flashcards from its word page, from Reading, from a quiz result or from Media used to be backed up only after its first review.
- **Reading and grammar pages count as study time:** time on the Reading screen and on a grammar point's page now counts toward today's minutes and your streak, like flashcards and quizzes. Scrolling and tapping keep the clock going; a pause of more than two minutes only counts as two.
- **Learning cards that come back count too:** after a session ends and a learning card becomes due again, the time you spend on it is now counted.
- **Quizzes:** tapping Next twice on the last question no longer saves the result twice, and the placement test can no longer get stuck on a spinner.
- **Delete account:** your phone's data is erased only after the account is really deleted. If deleting fails (for example, no connection), your progress stays on the phone.
- **Practice reminder:** the "reviews waiting" number no longer counts listening cards when you've turned listening cards off.
- **4 a.m. rollover:** Home and Flashcards update their "to review" and "new today" numbers at the 4 a.m. rollover even if the app stays open.
- Faster Flashcards screen and end-of-session screen (fewer database reads).
- **Fix:** the Grammar list sometimes opened scrolled to the bottom of the level. It now opens at the top.
- **Fix:** the Tests screen no longer says grammar and reading checks "arrive with those features"; it now points to where they are.
