## FluRai 0.12.0: Cloud backup (milestone 11)

### New
- **Your progress is backed up to your account** and comes back when you sign in on a new phone or after a reinstall: flashcards, streak days, test results, grammar progress, your channels, level and settings.
- Backup runs when you open the app, when you leave it, and before you sign out. **Me → Settings → Cloud backup** shows the last sync and has a **Sync now** button.
- Signing in on a phone with no data restores it before onboarding, so you aren't asked for your level again.
- Deleting your account also deletes your cloud backup.

### Notes
- Study time on two phones on the same day keeps the larger total; for flashcards and grammar the newer answer wins.
- The review history log is not backed up (nothing in the app reads it), and neither are cards you suspend or bury without reviewing them.
- Profile and settings are restored onto a phone that has none; changing them on one phone does not change them on another.
- The cloud database is Firestore in europe-west1; rules (firestore.rules) allow only the signed-in, email-confirmed owner.
- Needs an internet connection to back up; everything keeps working offline.
