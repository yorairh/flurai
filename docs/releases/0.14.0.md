# FluRai 0.14.0

Polish and speed.

- Home greets you with "Welcome" (ようこそ) until you have studied, then "Welcome back" (おかえり).
- Guests see "Back" instead of "Sign out" while choosing their level.
- The word list is loaded once and shared by progress, tests, reading and media, instead of four separate copies.
- Home, Progress and the streak only recalculate when your cards, tests or settings really changed.
- Cloud sync downloads its four collections at the same time and no longer re-uploads your settings and channels when they did not change.
