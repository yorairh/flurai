# FluRai 1.9.6

**Fixed (course screens)**
- **"Next" goes forward.** The button under a lesson opens the next entry in course order. "Continue where you left off" is a separate button, shown when it leads somewhere else. After the last lesson there is no Next button.
- **Trains and tables are never cut off silently.** A train or table wider than the screen shows a fade and an arrow at its right edge. Tables now use the full width, with columns sized to their content, and key points boxes are always full width.
- **Line breaking for Japanese.** A line never starts with a small kana (ゃ, っ, ー), 。、！？ or a closing bracket, and never ends with an opening bracket. A short ending such as 「た。」 stays together, and short Japanese words inside English text no longer split.
- **Example sentences use the whole card width**; the English line and the play button sit under the sentence.
- **No furigana in titles and headings**, and train captions are drawn larger so readings stay over their words.
- **Dialogues.** Lessons can now show a conversation between two speakers.
- **See also** links: lesson 27 now links to ばかりか and ばかりに, and lesson 28 to ようにする and ようになる.

**Course content (all 99 entries reviewed)**
- Every lesson opens with a short paragraph, explains each section before its examples, and has at least one train or table. Trains follow one convention (the particle is part of the noun car, Ø only for the zero carriage, one car per engine) and use the words "main carriage" and "engine" everywhere.
- Content fixes: -れる/-える rule in lesson 15, と in 8b, the は/も example in 14, 聞いてもらう in 66, やはり in 95, the うち reference in 91, こと endings in 64, and more. New teaching: のに (93), もう一度/もう少し (67), 〜かける (70), counter sound changes (71), たがる (9), ほどがある (94), そんなことない (74), the change-of-heart やっぱり (95).
- Lessons 11-14 use one heroine. Notes 45, 51, 52, 53 and 69 are now short reading pages with an original story, example sentences, trains and a table of the lessons each phrase comes from.
- Test questions: wrong and double-correct answers fixed, no more answers given away by the instruction or the option's form, no questions on grammar not yet taught, duplicates and drills replaced by questions on the lesson's idea, and every lesson has at least 22 questions (2,067 in all, none removed, no id changed).
