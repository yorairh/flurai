## FluRai 0.13.0: Conversation partner (milestone 12)

### New
- **Learn → Conversation partner:** chat in Japanese with an AI tutor (Google Gemini, through Firebase). Pick a situation: free chat, introducing yourself, a café, asking directions, shopping or the station.
- The tutor speaks at your level and prefers words and grammar you already know. It uses the grammar you find hard more often.
- After each message you send, a **Correction** appears under it when something was off, with the fix and a short explanation.
- Each reply has a **Listen** button (the phone's Japanese voice) and a **Show translation** button.
- Time in a conversation counts toward your daily study time and streak.

### One-time setup (already done if you read this in the app)
1. Firebase console → **AI Services → AI Logic → Get started** → choose **Gemini Developer API**.
2. In the same place, make sure **App Check** is not enforced for AI Logic (sideloaded builds can't pass it). A private app on the free plan is fine without it.

### Notes
- Needs an internet connection. Your messages go to Google to get a reply; FluRai doesn't save the conversation.
- Free-tier limits apply; if you hit them the chat says so and you can try again a few minutes later.
- Speaking to the tutor (microphone input) is not in this update. Mistakes are shown but do not yet feed your flashcards.
