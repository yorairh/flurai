# FluRai 0.13.0

Use FluRai without an account.

- **Continue without an account:** a new button on the welcome screen starts a guest profile. No email, no password, no verification.
- **Everything stays on the phone:** flashcards, streaks, tests, grammar and settings are saved on the device. There is no cloud backup in guest mode, and the Settings screen says so.
- **Move to an account later:** under Me, "Create an account or sign in" leaves guest mode (your guest data stays on the phone). After you sign in, a card offers to move the guest progress into your account.
- **Erase guest data:** in Settings you can delete everything the guest profile holds.
