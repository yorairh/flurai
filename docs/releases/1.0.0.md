# FluRai 1.0.0

The first release for friends. FluRai is an app for learning Japanese, measured against the JLPT levels N5 to N1.

**What you can do**
- **Flashcards:** about 8,000 words from N5 to N1 with readings, meanings and example sentences, scheduled by the same FSRS method Anki uses. Cards go word to meaning, meaning to word, and (optionally) hear-the-word. Start with hiragana and katakana, make your own decks, or study one level at a time.
- **Grammar:** 521 points from N5 to N1, each with an explanation, how it is built, examples, and practice questions.
- **Reading:** real sentences at your level, with furigana you can turn off. Tap any word for its meaning, or add it to your flashcards.
- **Tests:** a placement test that finds your level, 20-question checkpoints for each level (80% to pass), quick quizzes, and a listening quiz.
- **Progress:** the level the app thinks you are at, how much of each level's words and kanji you know, your weak words, and a 7-day forecast.
- **Streaks and reminders:** study time per day, streaks, and an optional daily reminder.
- **Explore:** video channels for each level, and anime, drama and book recommendations matched to what you know, with "learn the top words first".
- **Accounts:** sign up with email (you confirm it by link) and your progress is backed up, so a new phone picks up where you left off. Or use it as a guest, on this phone only, and move it into an account later.

**Good to know**
- Installing: open the APK on your phone and allow "install unknown apps" when asked. Later versions update over this one and keep your progress.
- Audio uses your phone's text-to-speech. If a Japanese voice is missing, the app offers to install it.
- Decks and channels are backed up as a whole: if you change decks on two phones at nearly the same time, the one that syncs last wins.
- Not included yet: the AI conversation partner.
