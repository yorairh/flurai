# FluRai 2.1.0

**New**
- Import Anki decks: Learn → Flashcards → "Import an Anki deck (.apkg)", or open a .apkg or .colpkg file with FluRai from your files or another app. Decks from every Anki version are read, including the new format of Anki 2.1.50 and later. A link takes you to the shared Japanese decks on AnkiWeb.
- Before importing you see what will come over: which field is the word, its reading and its meaning (with the first notes as a preview), what each card template becomes (Recognition, Production, Listening or left out), one deck or one per subdeck, and whether to keep your Anki progress.
- Words the dictionary knows use its readings, meanings and example sentences. Words and sentences it doesn't know are kept as your own words, marked "Your word". A sentence is never asked to be written (no Production card).
- Your Anki progress comes over: with FSRS on, each card's memory state is kept as it is; otherwise its review history is replayed. Suspended cards stay suspended. Cards that are already due can be spread over the next week or two. A word you have already answered in FluRai keeps its FluRai progress.
- Importing the same deck again adds only what is missing.

**Changed**
- Your own words are backed up with the rest of your progress. A phone still on 2.0.0 keeps syncing but doesn't show them until it is updated.
- The app is about 2.5 MB bigger (the decompressor for new Anki decks).
