# FluRai 1.8.0

One switch for furigana, and it now works across the app.

**New**
- **Furigana switch in Settings.** Me → Settings → Furigana turns readings above kanji on or off everywhere. The switch on the Reading page is gone: Reading follows this one.
- **Furigana in the grammar course.** Lesson paragraphs, key points, tables, train diagrams, lesson titles, and every test question, answer and explanation show readings above the kanji.
- **Furigana on words.** Word lists, the word page, deck lists, weak words and the flashcard answer put the reading above the kanji instead of on a separate line.

**Good to know**
- Flashcard fronts before you reveal, and questions that ask how a word is read, never show furigana, so they still test the reading.
- Course progress, flashcards and settings are untouched, and backup works as before.
