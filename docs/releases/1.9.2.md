# FluRai 1.9.2

Fixes to the grammar course found while playing it on a phone, in light and dark mode.

**Fixed**
- **The test result matches the lesson's best.** A lesson test also asks two questions from earlier lessons; the score and the pass mark now count only the lesson's own questions, and the review has its own line ("Review of earlier lessons: 2 of 2"). Before, the result said 9 / 10 while the list said "best 7 of 8".
- **Missed review questions clear.** Answering an earlier lesson's question right in a lesson test now removes it from that lesson's missed list.
- **Tests no longer repeat answers.** A lesson test has at most two questions with the same right answer (three "fill the gap" questions that all want を are gone).
- **Wrong reading of 行いて** in lesson 5 (it showed おこな instead of い).
- **Lesson 2's test no longer uses past tense**, which the course teaches in lesson 4.
- **Progress bars in dark mode** have a neutral track; an empty bar no longer looks like a full dark red one.
- **The train's couplings meet** in every lesson, and lesson 3's invisible carriage and が are two cars like in lesson 2.

**Improved**
- **Lesson pages:** furigana no longer makes a paragraph's lines wider apart than the next one; a word is no longer split across lines (投げ|る, ボ|ール, う|-row); a long reading over one kanji no longer leaves gaps on both sides; train captions have furigana; the summary line that repeated the first paragraph is gone; the lesson fills the screen and has a "Test" button in the top bar.
- **Tests:** one main button in a fixed place (Check, Next, then See result); a Correct / Not quite line with an icon for every kind of question; the gap shows the right answer after you answer and you can listen to the whole sentence; when another word order is also right it shows the usual order; a wrong answer shows "Right answer" in green; the pieces of "Build the sentence" stay in their places when you place one.
