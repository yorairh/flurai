# FluRai 2.1.2

**Fixed (course, lessons 3 to 10)**
- Every rule in lessons 3, 5, 7, 7.5, 8 and 10 now has an example sentence of its own, not just a word in a table.
- Lesson 3: a sentence shows that を disappears when the thing becomes the topic (このケーキは私が食べる).
- Lesson 5: sentences for ichidan verbs, the ぐ and す endings, 行く → 行った, the godan verbs that look ichidan (帰る), verbs ending in ある/うる/おる (作る), and the irregular する and 来る.
- Lesson 7: sentences for the う → わ negative, the ichidan negative, and いい → よかった / よくない.
- Lesson 7.5: the lesson now explains ましょう (the polite "let's"), which the test already asked about. ます and the longer ichidan helpers (させる, られる, よう) have sentences.
- Lesson 8: a sentence with へ.
- Lesson 10: a sentence with 来られる (read こられる).
