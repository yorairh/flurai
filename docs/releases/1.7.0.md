# FluRai 1.7.0

Grammar now has a recommended course.

**New**
- **Course tab.** Grammar opens on a new Course tab, after Cure Dolly's Organic Japanese: 99 entries in course order, each lesson written for FluRai with train diagrams, example sentences (furigana and audio) and a link to the original video. About 15 entries are short study notes without a test.
- **A test after every lesson.** Ten varied questions (choice, tap a word, arrange the pieces), each with a one-line explanation. Two come from earlier lessons you passed. Pass with 8 of 10; if you miss, see what went wrong and try again as often as you like.
- **Test yourself.** Pick 10, 20 or 30 random questions from the lessons you passed. Questions you missed before come up more often.
- **Open order.** Every lesson is open, a "Next lesson" card suggests where to go, and each lesson shows a small N5-N1 badge.
- **Links both ways.** Lessons list related reference points, and a reference point links back to its course lesson.

**Changed**
- The older lesson list is now the Reference tab, unchanged.

**Good to know**
- Course progress is saved and backed up separately. It never changes your level estimate or your Reference progress.
- Lessons are written in FluRai's own words; Cure Dolly is credited under Me → data sources.
