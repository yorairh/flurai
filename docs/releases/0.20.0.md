# FluRai 0.20.0

The full N5 and N4 grammar lists, and N3 grammar.

Milestones 19, 20 and 21 shipped together in this one release, so there is no 0.18.0 or 0.19.0.

- **N5 is now complete: 89 points** (64 new). The polite and plain forms, い- and な-adjectives, this/that/where words, existence and position, asking for things, going and direction, comparing, listing, time and frequency words, counters and telling time, wanting, and sentence endings such as ね and よ.
- **N4 grows to 108 points** (86 new): giving and receiving favours (てあげる, てもらう, ていただく), potential, causative and volitional forms, intending and planning (つもり, 予定), stages of an action (ところ, ばかり), guessing and hearsay (はず, らしい, みたい, そうだ), although and purpose (のに), advice (ばいい, たらいい), the commands, てみる, てしまう, てある, honorific and humble Japanese, and time words (間に, うちに, までに, たびに).
- **N3 is new: 158 points.** Reasons and results (せいで, おかげで, わけだ, わけがない, ものの, にもかかわらず), must, can't help and might (べき, ざるを得ない, ずにはいられない, かねない), the verb endings (がち, っぽい, かける, きる, 出す, っぱなし), formal comparisons and viewpoints (に対して, に比べて, にとって, によって), changes together (につれて, とともに), "based on" patterns, emphasis (さえ, こそ, なんか), casual speech (ちゃう, なきゃ, もん) and more.
- Every point has an explanation, how it is formed, two or three examples with English, and fill-in-the-blank practice, same as before. Your progress on earlier points is untouched.
- Learn → Grammar now lists N3, N4 and N5. N2 and N1 come later.
