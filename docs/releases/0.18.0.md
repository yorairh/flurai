# FluRai 0.18.0

The full N5 grammar list.

- **64 new N5 grammar points**, taking N5 from 25 to 89. They cover the polite and plain forms (です and でした, ます and ませんでした, plain past and present), adjectives (い and な, before nouns, past, negative, linking with くて and で), this/that/where words, existence (あります, います, where things are, position words), asking for things (をください, をお願いします), going and direction, comparing (より, のほうが, 一番), listing (や, か, も〜も), time and frequency words, counters and telling time, wanting (がほしい, たくない), and sentence endings such as ね and よ.
- Every point has an explanation, how it is formed, two or three examples with their English, and fill-in-the-blank practice, same as before. Your progress on the 25 earlier points is untouched.
- Open Learn → Grammar, pick N5, and tap a point to read it or practise just that point.
