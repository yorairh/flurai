## FluRai 0.10.0: Videos (milestone 9)

### New
- **Explore → Videos:** hand-picked YouTube channels for each level (N5 to N1), such as Japanese Ammo with Misa, Tokini Andy, Comprehensible Japanese and Nihongo no Mori. Tap one to open its videos on YouTube.
- **For the grammar you struggle with:** grammar points you get wrong more often than right show up with a one-tap video search.
- **Grammar → any point → Find videos about this**, a video search for that pattern.

### Notes
- Links open YouTube search results, so nothing breaks if a video is taken down. There is no in-app player or "watched" tracking yet.
- The channel list is bundled with the app, so changes arrive with updates.
