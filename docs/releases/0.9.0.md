## FluRai 0.9.0: Reading (milestone 8)

### New
- **Learn → Reading:** a set of six real Tatoeba sentences at the level you pick (N5 to N1), starting at your study level. "New set" draws another six.
- **Tap to look up:** underlined words open a sheet with the reading, meaning and level. Add the word to your flashcards from there, or open its full details and examples.
- **Show translations** switch, so you can try reading first.
- **Check yourself:** ten questions, "What does this sentence mean?", with the translations of other sentences as wrong options. Counts toward your study time and streak.
- **More to read:** links to NHK News Web Easy, Tadoku free readers and Watanoc (they open in the browser).

### Notes
- Tap-to-lookup finds words in their dictionary form only, so conjugated endings (食べた, 行きます) stay as plain text for now.
- Furigana over the sentences and audio-only flashcards are not in this update.
- Sentences are grouped by the hardest word they contain, so a set is a handful of separate sentences, not one story.
