## FluRai 0.11.0: Anime, shows & manga (milestone 10)

### New
- **Explore → Anime, shows & manga:** browse anime, dramas, movies, manga, novels and games from jiten.moe, filtered to the difficulty that suits your level (N5 to N1) and sorted by popularity. Covers load as you scroll, and more titles load at the end of the list.
- **How much you know:** open a title to see roughly what share of what is said you already know, based on its 200 most common words and your flashcards.
- **Learn the top words first:** one tap adds the 20 most common words of the title (that are in your JLPT lists and not started yet) to your flashcards.
- Links to AniList, MyAnimeList, IMDb and others where jiten.moe has them.
- The licences screen now credits jiten.moe.

### Notes
- This is the first feature that needs an internet connection. Everything else still works offline.
- Words outside the JLPT lists the app teaches count as unknown, so the shown figure is a little lower than the real one.
- Data is requested straight from jiten.moe and kept only in memory for 15 minutes. Please tell the jiten.moe team (Discord) that FluRai uses their public API for personal study.
