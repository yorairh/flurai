## FluRai 0.5.0: Progress dashboard (milestone 4)

The app now tells you where you stand, what you know, and what to work on.

### New
- **Home** shows today's plan (cards to review, new words left, a "Focus" word you've struggled with) and your estimated level.
- **Progress screen** (tap your level on Home, or Me → Progress):
  - **Your level and next goal**, with progress toward it. The estimate starts from the level you chose and moves up once you know 80% of the next level's words.
  - **Vocabulary by level:** for N5–N1, how many words are mature, known or still learning.
  - **Kanji by level:** kanji that appear in words you know.
  - **Improve now:** words you've forgotten before, worst first, with how well you'd recall them today.
  - **Coming up:** cards due over the next week.
- **Your words:** browse the words you've started by Learning, Known, Mature or Suspended, and by level. Tap a word for its page.

### How "known" works
A word counts as **known** once your memory of it is predicted to last a week, and **mature** at three weeks. Newer words are **learning**.

### Not yet
- Grammar progress (arrives with grammar lessons) and test-based level checks (the estimate only goes up from the level you chose until then).
- Custom decks are not scheduled yet.
