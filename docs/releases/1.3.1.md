# FluRai 1.3.1

A fix for furigana (the small kana over kanji).

**Fixed**
- 来ました showed the wrong reading over 来 (らい～ instead of き). The same kind of mistake is gone from the other sentences too: 彼の is read かれの, 今日は is read きょうは, 書いて is read かいて, and so on.
- A lot of furigana was missing. About one kanji in five had none, mostly in verbs like 見ました, 持って or 行った. Now almost every kanji in example sentences, Reading and grammar examples has its reading.
- Names such as 山田 and 田中 now have furigana, and お客様 is read おきゃくさま.

**Good to know**
- The readings are now worked out in advance from a dictionary instead of guessed on your phone, so the app's data is a little bigger (about 2 MB).
- Your progress, decks and settings are untouched.
- If you still find a wrong reading, tell me the sentence and where you saw it.
