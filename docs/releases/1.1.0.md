# FluRai 1.1.0

The first update after the friends release, built from a full play-test of 1.0.0. It fixes the flashcard order, makes your level mean something, and adds sentence audio.

**New**
- **Listen to example sentences.** A play button sits next to example sentences on the flashcard answer, the word page, grammar examples and Reading. Tap to hear it, press and hold to hear it slowly. It uses your phone's Japanese voice.

**Better flashcards**
- Cards no longer come in the same order every time, within a session or from day to day.
- A card you just answered, or another card of the same word, waits at least five cards before it comes back. The hear-the-word card no longer arrives right after the card that gives its answer away: it unlocks a day later.
- Words you add yourself (from a word page, Reading or a recommended title) show as new, not as reviews.
- The three counters at the top of a session are labeled New, Learning and Review.

**A level you can trust**
- Your level is now worked out from what you know: a level counts when you pass its checkpoint, or know 80% of its words and 80% of its grammar. The level you picked when you signed up, and a placement result, no longer set it.
- Level checkpoints now have 30 questions: words, kanji readings and grammar. A passed older checkpoint still counts.
- The placement test starts at N5, asks words and grammar, and suggests where to start. Take that level's checkpoint to confirm it.
- Home and Me say "Studying from N5" for the word list you learn from; "Your level" is the one the app worked out.
- Progress shows your grammar for every level and which checkpoints you passed. Opening Progress from Me keeps the Me tab selected.

**Good to know**
- If you passed a checkpoint before, your level may look different now: it only counts what the app can see.
- Level checkpoints have more questions, so they take a few minutes longer.
- Still to come in 1.2.0: Reading sentences that really match their level, better example sentences, and better word search.
