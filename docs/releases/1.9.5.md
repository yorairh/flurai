# FluRai 1.9.5

**Fixed (course screens)**
- **"Next" goes forward.** The button under a lesson opens the next entry in course order. "Continue where you left off" is a separate button, shown when it leads somewhere else. After the last lesson there is no Next button.
- **Trains and tables are never cut off silently.** A train or table wider than the screen shows a fade and an arrow at its right edge. Tables now use the full width, with columns sized to their content, and key points boxes are always full width.
- **Line breaking for Japanese.** A line never starts with a small kana (ゃ, っ, ー), 。、！？ or a closing bracket, and never ends with an opening bracket. A short ending such as 「た。」 stays together, and short Japanese words inside English text no longer split.
- **Example sentences use the whole card width**; the English line and the play button sit under the sentence.
- **No furigana in titles and headings**, and train captions are drawn larger so readings stay over their words.
- **Dialogues.** Lessons can now show a conversation between two speakers.
- **See also** links: lesson 27 now links to ばかりか and ばかりに, and lesson 28 to ようにする and ようになる.
