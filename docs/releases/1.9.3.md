# FluRai 1.9.3

The course tests are now a full 10 questions, and a checkup comes after every five lessons.

**Changed**
- **A lesson test asks 10 questions, all about that lesson.** The two questions from earlier lessons are gone, so a test is no longer 8 questions plus a review.
- **A checkup after every fifth lesson.** Lessons 1 to 5, 6 to 10 and so on each have a checkup of 20 questions, four from each of its five lessons, the ones you missed before first. It opens when you have passed all five lessons, is passed at 80%, and keeps your best score. The Course tab lists the checkups you have opened and the next one to earn.
