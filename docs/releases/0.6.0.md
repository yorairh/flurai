## FluRai 0.6.0: Study time, streaks and reminders (milestone 5)

FluRai now keeps track of how much you study, rewards showing up every day, and can nudge you to practise.

### New
- **Study time:** the time you spend on flashcards is counted per day. The clock pauses when the app goes to the background, and a pause longer than two minutes between taps only counts two minutes.
- **Streak card on Home:** a ring showing today's minutes against your daily goal, your current and best day streak, this week's total, and a five-week heat map. In the evening it warns you when your streak is about to lapse, and it cheers at 7, 30, 100 and 365 days.
- **Settings** (Me → Settings):
  - **Daily reminder** on/off and the time. It is skipped if you have already studied enough today, and the text adapts ("12 reviews waiting. Keep your 4-day streak going!"). Android 13+ asks for notification permission when you switch it on.
  - **Streak rule:** how many minutes make a day count (1, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 30; default 5).
  - **Study goals** shortcut and the **app version**.
- **Learn more:** when you finish your session you can keep going with 10 more new words, or pick any level (N5 to N1).

### Fixes
- The version label would have crashed on Android 8.0 and 8.1.

### Notes
- Streak freezes and a separate "streak at risk" notification are not built yet; the evening warning appears on Home instead.
- Theme choice is not included yet.
- Only flashcard time is counted for now; grammar, reading and tests will count once they exist.
