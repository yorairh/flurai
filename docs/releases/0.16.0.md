# FluRai 0.16.0

Listening practice.

- **Listening cards:** once you have learned a word, a new flashcard type unlocks for it (from the next day). It plays the word out loud and you say what it is; tap Show answer to see the word, its reading and meaning. The audio starts as the card appears, and the big play button repeats it.
- **Listening quiz:** a new card on the Tests screen. Ten spoken words, pick what each one means. The written word shows after you answer. Missed words can be added to your flashcards like in the other quizzes.
- **Setting:** Me → Settings → Flashcards → "Listening cards" turns the new card type on or off. Off hides the cards you already have and stops new ones from being added; turning it back on brings them back.
- The word page lists a word's listening card next to its other cards.
- The speech engine now remembers a word asked for while it is still starting up, so the first card of a session is not silent.
