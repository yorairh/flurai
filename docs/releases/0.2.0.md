## FluRai 0.2.0: Accounts and onboarding (milestone 1)

### New
- **Accounts** (Firebase): create an account with your name, email and password; sign in, sign out, and reset a forgotten password by email
- **Onboarding:**
  - pick your starting level (Beginner, N5–N1)
  - beginners are asked whether they can read hiragana and katakana
  - set a daily goal (minutes, new words per day)
- **Home** greets you by name and shows your level and goals
- **Me tab:** your account, plus **Edit level & goals** to change your choices any time

### Fixed during testing
- Goal pills on Home now wrap instead of squeezing the last one
