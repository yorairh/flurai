- Godan verbs that look ichidan (帰る, 走る, 入る) have their own sub-section with a table.
- The godan table is narrower, so each example fits on one line.
- The train is smaller and fits the screen, with the zero carriage and the を carriage in place.
- The confusing "memory hooks" box is gone.
- The test names the verb in its fill-the-gap questions (待つ, 書く, 遊ぶ) so you never have to guess a verb you have not met.
- Furigana: 食べて and 食べた are read たべて and たべた (they were read しょくべて).
