## FluRai 0.7.0: Tests (milestone 6)

You can now test yourself, and the results shape your level.

### New
- **Learn → Tests**, with three kinds of test:
  - **Placement test:** adaptive vocabulary questions, five per level, starting at N4. Four right moves you up a level, fewer moves you down; it stops when it has found the hardest level you pass (usually 10 to 25 questions). The result can be set as your study level with one tap.
  - **Level checkpoints (N5 to N1):** 20 questions on one level's words (meaning) and kanji (reading). Score 80% to pass. Your best score per level is shown.
  - **Quick quiz:** ten questions mixing words you already study with new ones from your level.
- **Results screen:** your score, and a button to add the words you missed to your flashcards.
- **Your level estimate** (Home and Progress) now takes a placement result or a passed checkpoint into account, so it can start higher than the level you chose at sign-up.
- Test time counts toward your daily study time and streak.

### Notes
- Tests cover vocabulary and kanji readings for now. Grammar and reading questions arrive with those features.
- Wrong answers are not yet fed into the "Improve now" list; they can be added to flashcards instead.
