# FluRai 1.2.0

Better content: Reading sentences that really match their level, easier example sentences, and a search that finds what you type.

**Reading**
- Sentences are now sorted by their hardest word and their hardest grammar, so N5 and N4 no longer show slang, polite-speech or passive sentences. There are many more sentences to read at every level.
- 私 is shown as わたし, and the comprehension check offers wrong answers that share a word with the sentence, so one known word is not enough to guess it.
- Furigana lines are tighter, and a screen reader reads each sentence as one piece, with a "Look up" action for each word.

**Examples and words**
- Example sentences are chosen to be no harder than the word they teach.
- ええ ("yes") no longer shows sentences with the Kansai ええ ("good"), and words like ああ, いくら and これ now match the right dictionary entry.
- Example sentences on flashcards, word pages and grammar pages show furigana (follows the furigana setting).
- お母さん, お父さん, お兄さん and お姉さん say "polite" instead of "formal".
- Media: "Learn the top words first" now lists the words before adding them, and leaves out words well above your level (with an "Include harder words" switch).

**Search and the library**
- Search puts the best match first: "eat" finds 食べる before "weather", and "cat" finds 猫 first.
- You can type romaji: "taberu" and "neko" find the words.
- The Kanji tab has a search by character, meaning or reading (try "ookii").
- The kanji sheet shows readings like おお(きい) and lists the words written with the kanji. Kanji on a word page are tappable.
- A custom deck has an "Add words" button.

**Good to know**
- This update replaces the built-in word and sentence data. Your progress, decks and settings are untouched.
- Still to come in 1.3.0: better quiz answers and results, and kana combination sounds.
