# FluRai 1.3.0

Better quizzes and grammar practice, all the kana, and a clearer streak card. This finishes the changes that came out of the play-test of 1.0.0.

**Quizzes**
- Wrong answers in word questions are now the same kind of word at the same level, so they are harder to guess. Meanings are no longer cut off in the middle of a word.
- The level checkpoint asks how a word written with kanji is read (生きる, not a single kanji), so the okurigana is part of the question. A missed one can go to your flashcards.
- When a test ends you see every question you missed, with the right answer and what you picked. "Add missed words" only counts words that are not in your flashcards yet.
- Leaving a test in the middle asks first, because the answers are not saved.

**Grammar practice**
- Wrong options look like the right one: a particle among particles, an ending among endings.
- After an answer you see the point and the first sentence of its explanation.
- Most grammar points have extra practice sentences, so practice is no longer only the examples you just read. They come from Tatoeba and appear only in practice.
- Fixes: the かもしれません answers, American spelling, and あげく, だけあって and ざるを得ない moved from N3 to N2.

**Kana**
- Hiragana and katakana now include the combination sounds (きゃ, しゅ, ちょ ...). The deck screen is a chart, with the kana you started shaded.
- A kana card shows a simple word that starts with that kana.

**Home and settings**
- The streak ring shows your streak in days. The heat map has weekday labels and a legend.
- Settings explains that the streak minutes and the daily goal are separate.
- Small wording changes in Videos and Media.

**Good to know**
- This update replaces the built-in word, sentence and grammar data. Your progress, decks and settings are untouched. New kana start as new cards.
- Not included yet: the AI conversation partner.
