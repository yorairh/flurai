## FluRai 0.4.0: Flashcards (milestone 3)

Anki-style spaced repetition for words and kana.

### New
- **FSRS-6 scheduling** (the algorithm modern Anki uses), tested to match the official implementation
- **Learn → Flashcards:**
  - see today's reviews and new words, then **Study now**
  - or study a specific deck: N5–N1, hiragana or katakana
- **New words:**
  - come from your level, in Genki lesson order first
  - beginners who can't read kana start with hiragana, then katakana
  - the daily limit comes from your goal
- **Reviewing:**
  - Show answer, then rate Again / Hard / Good / Easy; each button shows when the card returns
  - the answer shows the meaning, reading and an example sentence, and plays Japanese audio
  - hide a card until tomorrow, or suspend it
- **Two directions:** Japanese → meaning first; meaning → Japanese unlocks once a word is learned
- **Word page:** play audio, see your card status ("Review · in 12d"), start learning, suspend or reset
- Your progress is stored on the phone, per account (cloud sync comes in a later version)
