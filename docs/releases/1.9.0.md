# FluRai 1.9.0

Grammar course tests now check that you understood the idea, not just the examples.

**New**
- **New sentences in every test.** Each lesson now has 20 to 24 questions, all on sentences and words that are not in the lesson. They use common N5-N4 words, so a test checks the grammar, not the vocabulary. About 2,000 questions in all (1,080 before, mostly built from the lesson examples).
- **Translation after you answer.** For a question on a Japanese sentence, the English appears under the answer, next to the explanation, so it never gives the answer away.
- **More than one right word order.** When an "arrange the pieces" question has several natural orders, any of them counts as correct.
- Better questions: every question was answered blind by a second reviewer, and the ambiguous ones were fixed or rewritten.

**Good to know**
- Retries and "Test yourself" now draw from a bigger pool, so you see different sentences.
- Your course progress and best scores are kept. Questions you missed in a lesson that were replaced are simply dropped from "Test yourself".
