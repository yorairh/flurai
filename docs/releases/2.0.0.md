# FluRai 2.0.0

**New**
- Make a flashcard deck from any anime, show, book or game in Explore → Media. Choose how much of the title you want to understand (80%, 90% or 95% of its text, or its top 200, 500 or 1,000 words, or all of them), leave out words you know, words that appear only once, kana-only words or JLPT words well above your level, and keep the words in order of frequency or in story order. Words outside the JLPT lists come in too, from the dictionary. Making the deck again later adds only the words it is missing.
- New words can come from one of your decks: in Me → Edit level & goals, pick a deck under "Take new words from a deck first" (or switch it on when you make a deck from a title). Your daily number of new words stays the same; when the deck has no new words left, they come from your level's list again.
- "How much you would understand" for a title now counts every word found in the dictionary, not only the JLPT words.

**Changed**
- Decks are backed up one by one, so a deck made on one phone and a deck made on another are both kept, and big decks back up quickly.
- Dictionary words whose kanji spellings are only used in searches are now shown in kana (the particle の was shown as 乃).
- A very large backup is uploaded a part at a time (up to 2,000 changes per sync). Settings shows how many are still waiting.

**Before you update**
- If you sync on more than one phone, update all of them: after this version syncs, FluRai 1.10.0 and older ask to be updated before they sync again. Nothing on those phones is lost.
