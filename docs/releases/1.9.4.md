# FluRai 1.9.4

**Fixed**
- **Lesson 3 showed the invisible carriage as ゼロ** in one example (ボールは私がさくらに（ゼロを）投げる). It is now drawn as Ø like everywhere else in the course.
- **Trains in the lessons had cars of different heights** when some cars had furigana (for example "Is eating" in lesson 4). Every car of a train is now the same height, so the couplings meet.
- **Furigana spacing:** a line that starts with a kanji word no longer starts indented (the train captions and the tables in lessons 5 and 8b), and readings of neighboring words no longer run into each other.
- Two test questions used grammar from later lessons: lesson 2 (見ている, now 見る) and lesson 3 (くれた, now a sentence with 投げる).

**Clearer lessons for beginners**
- **Lesson 4** now shows how the past is made, with a table of common verbs in the non-past, past and て-form, instead of only one example with 食べる.
- **Lesson 5** names the verb kinds (ichidan, godan, irregular) instead of numbering them, since textbooks number them differently. It explains how to spot an ichidan verb (an い or え sound before る), and adds tables for ichidan and irregular verbs with their past forms.
- **Lesson 7** explains the past of だ (だった) and ends with tables of all the forms together: non-past, negative, past and negative past for verbs, い-adjectives and nouns.
- **Lesson 7.5** says in plain English what each helper word means (ない "not", せる "make someone do", and so on).
- **Lesson 9** shows how to make the い-stem and adds a table of common verbs with たい, and 食べ is read た in the explanation.
- **Fewer examples:** lessons 1–10 show at most 5 example sentences each (lesson 6 had 12), keeping the clearest one for each idea.
