# FluRai 1.5.0

Learn all the words of a show, not just the first 20.

**New**
- **Save all its words as a deck.** A title's page in Explore → Media now has one button that saves every word of the show that the app teaches as a deck, most common first. It starts no flashcards until you study the deck.
- **Your progress on the show's words.** The page shows "You know X of Y words" with a progress bar, counted over the whole show's vocabulary.

**Changed**
- **A simpler title page.** The "About N% of what is said" headline, the 200-word figures, the list of 20 words, "Learn these words first", "Save them as a deck" and the "Include harder words" switch are gone.
- **Decks keep their order.** A deck made from a list now keeps the order of that list.

**Good to know**
- Opening a title reads its whole word list from jiten.moe, a few seconds and a few requests, then keeps it for 15 minutes. If jiten.moe is busy you see "Try again".
- Only words from the JLPT lists the app teaches can go into a deck, so it holds fewer words than the title's total.
- Your progress, decks and settings are untouched, and backup works as before.
