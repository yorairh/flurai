# FluRai 0.22.0

Grammar for every level, N5 to N1.

Milestones 19 to 23 shipped together in this one release, so there is no 0.18.0, 0.19.0, 0.20.0 or 0.21.0.

- **Level tabs in Grammar:** the Grammar screen now has N5 to N1 tabs like the Reading screen, so you see one level at a time. It opens on your own level, and each tab starts at the top of its list.
- **521 grammar points in total:** N5 89, N4 108, N3 158, N2 95, N1 71. Every point has an explanation, how it is formed, two or three examples with English, and fill-in-the-blank practice, as before. Your progress on earlier points is untouched.
- **N5 is complete (64 new points):** the polite and plain forms, い- and な-adjectives, this/that/where words, existence and position, asking for things, going and direction, comparing, listing, time and frequency words, counters and telling time, wanting, and sentence endings such as ね and よ.
- **N4 grows from 22 to 108:** favors (てあげる, てもらう, ていただく), potential, causative and volitional forms, つもり and 予定, はず, らしい, みたい, のに, honorific and humble Japanese, time words and more.
- **N3 is new (158 points):** reasons and results, must / can't help / might, verb endings (がち, っぽい, かける, きる), formal comparisons and viewpoints, "based on" patterns, emphasis words, casual speech and more.
- **N2 is new (95 points):** formal connectors (に際して, をきっかけに, をめぐって, に伴って), "regardless of" patterns, concessions (とはいえ, からといって), "not to mention" (はもとより, のみならず), strong negatives and warnings (ないではおかない, ざるを得ない style), the casual-but-strict endings (っこない, てはいられない) and more.
- **N1 is new (71 points):** literary and formal patterns such as あっての, 極まりない, ならでは, にあるまじき, をもって, をおいて, たりとも, ようものなら and the "the moment" family (やいなや, が早いか). N1 is the least complete level and will grow.
- The examples are written for this app, not copied from textbooks, and have not been independently reviewed. Tell me about anything that reads wrong.
