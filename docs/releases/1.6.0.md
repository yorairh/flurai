# FluRai 1.6.0

Your own decks now look and work like the built-in ones.

**New**
- **Progress on every deck.** A custom deck shows "X of N started" with a progress bar.
- **See what you already study.** Words that already have a flashcard are shaded and marked "started", so you can tell why a word shows up in your reviews.
- **Edit mode.** The pencil in the top bar turns editing on: rename the deck, delete the deck, or remove a word with the X next to it.

**Changed**
- Outside Edit mode the word list has no X buttons, so you cannot remove a word by accident.

**Good to know**
- Removing a word or deleting a deck only changes the list. Your flashcards and progress are kept.
- Your progress, decks and settings are untouched, and backup works as before.
