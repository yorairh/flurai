## FluRai 0.3.0: Word library (milestone 2)

The app now ships with its own Japanese study content, built from open data.

### New
- **Built-in content:**
  - 8,025 JLPT words (N5 717 · N4 665 · N3 2,139 · N2 1,806 · N1 2,698) with readings, meanings, parts of speech and Genki lesson numbers
  - 2,211 kanji with on/kun readings, meanings and stroke counts
  - 16,577 example sentences with English translations (86% of words have up to three)
- **Learn → Word library:**
  - search by kanji, kana or English
  - browse words by level, with a word page showing its examples
  - a kanji grid per level, with readings
- **Me → Data sources & licences:** credits for JMdict/KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG), the JLPT lists (Jonathan Waller), and Tatoeba
